# Google Flow accounts and rules → rulebook

All Google Flow use (accounts, project ids, failover chain, pacing, the "unusual activity" rule, cool-downs, batch runner, troubleshooting) follows the Flow rulebook:
**`google-flow/google-flow-accounts.md`** (runner: `run_batch.py` + `batch_manifest.example.py` in the same folder; your profiles stay private in `~/.gflow/profiles.json`). Read it before any Flow call. Don't copy its rules here.
