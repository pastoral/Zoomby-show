# Storyboards and shot lists (premium)

## Initial storyboard: `01_storyboard/storyboard_initial.md`
Written after Round 1. One block per scene:
```
### Scene 3 · badge 6 · "Messi piggyback" · outfit barca
LINE:     Messi's first Barcelona goal? Assisted by Ronaldinho, who then carried him like a dad at a theme park.
LOCATION: floodlit night stadium
BEATS:    1) set-up: young teammate scores  2) detail: thumbs-up  3) punchline: piggyback across the pitch
CAST:     young teammate (cartoon head, long brown hair, same kit)
PROPS:    ball, goal
NUMBER:   none            · BUBBLE: BIG BRO
```
Rules:
- 3–4 visual beats per fact: set-up (wide/medium) → detail (POV, insert, top-down) → reaction (face close-up) → punchline.
- Write drawable nouns: every beat must be one clear picture.
- Outfit per scene (e.g. national kit for national-team facts, club kit for club facts).
- Numbers only for real figures from the verified script (shown later as number pops).
- End the file with the **outfit list** and the **cast list** (each cast member described by looks only).
- Mirror into `zoomby.project.json → storyboard_initial`.

## Shot list: `01_storyboard/shotlist.md` (after Round 2)
A table of every shot, built from the chosen Round 2 option per scene:

| # | Time (s) | Words | Framing | Picture | Face / camera / FX |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3b | 26.9–28.5 | "Assisted by Ronaldinho…" | medium | Him in the Barcelona kit, thumbs-up, delighted | eye darts |

- Shot IDs: `H1…` for the hook, `<scene><letter>` (`1a`, `1b`…), `E1…` for the ending; they become the image file names.
- Times are estimates until the voice-over is aligned (190 wpm); afterwards cut on cue words.
- Every face shot names its expression; every punchline names its punch / FX.

## Final storyboard + script
- `storyboard_final.md`: the shot list per scene plus the locked line, any **CHANGED** line with a one-line reason, bubbles, number pops, outfit.
- `script_final.md`: hook, lines with connectors, ending, word count, estimated length (words ÷ 190 × 60 s), pronunciation hints. The user's approval **locks the script**; the voice-over is recorded from this file.
